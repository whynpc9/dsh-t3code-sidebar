Keep the native sidebar workflow while grouping sessions by status, workspace, or one list.

Use the controls below for the sidebar display, off-peak snooze, automatic settling and workspace policies, or return here anytime from **View options → Sidebar settings…** in the sidebar.

During DeepSeek peak pricing hours, **Snooze → Until off-peak** hides a session until the half-price hours begin; it never sends a prompt or pauses the agent. For an idle session, **Resume at off-peak…** additionally has the Harness send a message you write at that time, as a scheduled task listed under **Automation tasks**; returning early cancels it. Chinese public holidays come from [holiday-cn](https://github.com/NateScarlet/holiday-cn) (MIT) and cover 2026–2027; refresh them with `pnpm run holidays:update`.
